Generated by `uv run python -m wikisearch.ingest.report` from the Italian Wikinews dump of 20 March 2025 (Wikimedia Enterprise HTML, `itwikinews-NS0-20250320`). Every number below is recomputed from the dump with the parser of `wikisearch/ingest/parse.py`: the report describes exactly the documents that are indexed. Base of Daniel's slides (the data and its challenges, the indexing choices).

## 2. The challenges, with numbers and one real example each

Each item: what makes the data hard, how much of the corpus it touches, a real page, what we did, and why.

### 2.1 The same page appears more than once

25 page ids appear on more than one line (48 extra lines): 22 twice, the others up to 13 times. Example: "Inaugurazione a Grottaferrata della scultura "La violenza è una gabbia" di Anna Izzo" (id 53678) is on 13 lines, one per revision. **We keep the version with the latest `date_modified`**: the dump is a snapshot, and older revisions would be near-identical documents competing in the ranking.

### 2.2 The news date is not a field

The dump has `date_modified` (the last edit, often years later) but no publication date. The date is written by hand in a template at the top of the wikitext, in Italian, in two templates: `{{data}}` (10,918 pages) and `{{luogodata}}` (769, with the place). Example, "Belgio: operatori sanitari voltano le spalle al primo ministro":

    {{luogodata|mercoledì|luogo=Bruxelles|data=20 maggio 2020}}

becomes `date: 2020-05-20`. Without a template we fall back to the daily category ("Prima serata televisiva 14 febbraio 2011", category `14 febbraio 2011` -> `2011-02-14`).

Result: 11,687 dates from the template, 40 from the category, 486 documents without a date (82 of them published). `date_source` records where each date comes from. **Why it matters:** the date filter and the time-bound queries of the `manual` set ("elezioni 2008") depend on it.

### 2.3 The dump's abstract is not a usable lead

The dump has an `abstract` field, but 11,632 abstracts start with the date line and 1,957 are under 40 characters. Example, "Italia: concorso n. 73/2008 del SuperEnalotto": the abstract is only "martedì 17 giugno 2008". **We build `lead` from the HTML** (first real paragraph after the date): "Concorso n. 73: 2.835.826,54 euro". The lead is what the embeddings see (title + lead), so a date in its place would make every vector of the same day look alike.

### 2.4 Categories mix topics, places, dates and maintenance

5,963 distinct categories in the dump, 100,562 assignments. 4,188 of them are daily date categories (one per day, 12,226 assignments) and 22 are maintenance categories (25,003 assignments: `Pubblicati` 11,032, `Articoli da controllare per l'archiviazione` 7,502, `Articoli archiviati` 4,133). Example, "Il videogioco This War of Mine farà parte delle letture delle scuole polacche":

    dump:    Videogiochi, 18 giugno 2020, Articoli da controllare per l'archiviazione, Pubblicati, Europa, Polonia, Cultura e società, Istruzione
    indexed: Videogiochi, Europa, Polonia, Cultura e società, Istruzione

After cleaning: 1,753 categories, 63,333 assignments. Top: Europa 8,518, Italia 6,743, Sport 4,087, Calcio 2,451, Cultura e società 2,070, Politica e conflitti 2,013. 419 categories are used by a single page. **Why:** categories are a facet in the interface; maintenance labels and dates (already in `date`) would fill the facet with noise.

### 2.5 The same newspaper is written in many ways

Sources name their agency by hand: 2,965 different spellings. The top groups:

| agency (name kept) | sources | spellings in the dump |
|---|---|---|
| la Repubblica | 1,911 | `la Repubblica` 1,370, `La Repubblica` 357, `Repubblica` 70, `repubblica.it` 46, `Repubblica.it` 43, `la Repubblica.it` 7, +8 more |
| Corriere della Sera | 1,547 | `Corriere della Sera` 1,379, `Corriere della sera` 67, `Il Corriere della Sera` 40, `Corriere.it` 25, `corriere.it` 18, `Corriere Della Sera` 4, +7 more |
| ANSA | 1,542 | `ANSA` 1,204, `Ansa` 179, `ansa.it` 129, `Ansa.it` 21, `ANSA.it` 7, `ansa` 1, +1 more |
| La Gazzetta dello Sport | 1,074 | `La Gazzetta dello Sport` 562, `Gazzetta dello Sport` 313, `gazzetta.it` 75, `la Gazzetta dello Sport` 69, `Gazzetta.it` 37, `La Gazzetta dello sport` 8, +2 more |
| Lega Serie A | 428 | `Lega Serie A` 422, `Legaseriea.it` 6 |

**We normalise with a key** (lowercase, no accents, no leading article, no `.it`/`.com`, no punctuation) plus 14 explicit aliases (`Corriere.it` -> Corriere della Sera, `AGI` = Agenzia Giornalistica Italia, ...), and display the most frequent spelling of each group: 2,965 -> 2,304 agencies, 345 of which merged more than one spelling. Most used afterwards (documents): la Repubblica 1,728, Corriere della Sera 1,425, ANSA 1,371, La Gazzetta dello Sport 914, Lega Serie A 425, Federazione Rugby Italia 307. **Why:** the agency is a filter in the interface; without this, filtering on `la Repubblica` would miss 28% of its sources.

### 2.6 Template-generated near-duplicates

Many pages are filled from the same template: same title up to the numbers, almost the same text. 76 title patterns (digits replaced by `#`) have 5 or more pages, 2,742 documents in all (22% of the corpus). The largest:

| pattern (`series_key`) | pages |
|---|---|
| Calcio, Serie B #-#: #ª giornata | 412 |
| Calcio, Serie A #-#: #ª giornata | 410 |
| Italia: concorso n. #/# del SuperEnalotto | 216 |
| Calcio, Scottish Premiership #-#, Stagione regolare: #ª giornata | 99 |
| Calcio Italia campionato nazionale Serie C# girone A #-# #° giornata andata | 98 |

Example: "Italia: concorso n. 128/2007 del SuperEnalotto"; "Italia: concorso n. 129/2007 del SuperEnalotto"; "Italia: concorso n. 130/2007 del SuperEnalotto". **We keep them** (they are real news) and store `series_key`, so the interface can collapse a series into one result (stretch experiment E7). They also make some queries hard: "risultati SuperEnalotto" has 216 almost equally good answers.

### 2.7 Not every page is a news article

1,181 documents (10%) are not in `Categoria:Pubblicati`:

1. other (sport round reports in prose, news never reviewed, drafts): 682
1. pages with tables (results, standings, calendars): 294
1. special pages ("Speciale ..." hubs and their subpages): 167
1. daily TV schedules: 38

Only 777 of them have a date; median length 930 characters. **We keep them, flagged with `published: false`** (decided 16 Nov): some answer real queries (a standings table, an election special), and the evaluation needs every page. Whether to rank them lower is experiment E5 (boost on `published`).

### 2.8 36% of the text is in tables

3,661 documents (3,219 published) contain tables: match reports, standings, lottery draws, TV schedules. Tables hold 36% of all the text of the corpus, and 366 documents (237 published) are nothing but tables. **We keep tables as text, one line per row** (decided 16 Nov; the brief first said paragraphs and lists only): dropping them would leave those pages empty and unfindable.

### 2.9 Leakage: the test queries are written inside the pages

Our known-item test set (`fonti`) uses the titles of the sources as queries: headlines written by other newspapers about the same event. Those titles are inside each page, in the `{{fonte}}` templates. Example, "Texas: scagionato da una violenza sessuale dopo 25 anni di carcere":

    {{fonte
    |url=http://it.euronews.net/2011/01/28/il-dna-lo-scagiona-ma-ha-gia-scontato-24-anni/
    |titolo=Il Dna lo scagiona, ma ha già scontato 24 anni
    |agenzia=euronews
    |data=28 gennaio 2011
    }}

Query: "Il Dna lo scagiona, ma ha già scontato 24 anni". It shares only `anni` with the title, and the `italian` analyzer does not help: its light stemmer gives `scagion` for "scagiona" and `scagionat` for "scagionato". Searching the title alone cannot find the article; the body can, through `24`, `anni`, `dna`. This is why we search title, lead and body together, and why queries that share no word at all are left to the embeddings (experiment E3). If the source title were searchable, the page would contain the query word for word and the test would measure nothing.

**We remove every source template from `body`**, not only the "Fonti" section: in 381 pages at least one source is not in a "Fonti" section (mostly under "Collegamenti esterni"), and even dropping every section the parser drops by heading ("Fonti", "Collegamenti esterni", "Note", ...) would leave sources in 19 pages: with no heading at all, like "Ligue 1 2012-2013: il PSG pareggia in Corsica a reti bianche. Lyon, OM, Bastia e Bordeaux in testa", in the text before the "Fonti" heading, or under headings such as "Riferimenti" and "Aggiornamenti". Check: the body still contains a source title (4+ words) verbatim in 51 documents; read by hand, they are natural (the name of an exhibition or a play quoted in both), not source text left behind. The titles are kept in a separate `sources_text` field, searched only by `bm25-italian-leak`, to measure how much leakage would inflate the results (experiment E4).

## 3. The indices

Two indices with the same fields and two text analyses (experiment E1): `standard` (tokenise, lowercase) and the built-in `italian` (elision, Italian stop words, light stemmer: `elezioni` = `elezione`, `l'Italia` = `Italia`). 1 shard, 0 replicas, Elasticsearch 9.5.0 with a 1 GB heap on an 8 GB laptop. Means of the last three builds (`results/indexing.csv`):

| index | vectors | builds | seconds (mean) | size (MB) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `wikinews-italian` | no | 3 | 3.8 | 35.7 |
| `wikinews-standard` | no | 3 | 4.2 | 38.2 |
| `wikinews-italian` | yes | 3 | 13.8 | 54.9 |
| `wikinews-standard` | yes | 3 | 14.9 | 57.4 |

1. Without vectors, `italian` is 6% smaller than `standard`: stop words are gone and stems merge word forms, so fewer distinct terms and postings.
2. Vectors (e5-small, 384 dimensions, one per document from title + lead) make the build 3.6x slower (the HNSW graph) and add 19.2 MB, about the size of the raw float32 vectors (12,213 x 384 x 4 bytes = 18.8 MB): `index_options: hnsw` keeps them unquantized. Quantization (`int8_hnsw`, `bbq_hnsw`) is the stretch experiment E8.
3. The first build is slower (JVM warm-up): that is why every build is repeated three times.

## 4. Known limits

1. 486 documents have no date (mostly specials and non-published pages): a date filter hides them.
2. The agency key removes a leading article even without a space (needed for `ilpost.it`, `LaStampa`): it merges two pairs it should not (`VG` with `IVG`, `post.com` with Il Post), one or two sources each.
3. The agency key keeps only ASCII letters and digits: 9 names in Cyrillic or made of punctuation (`-`), 18 sources, are not normalised and stay as written.
4. Entities are the Wikipedia links of the text and include noise from templates of match reports: `Central European Time` (720 pages), `Central European Summer Time` (665 pages), `Calcio di rigore` (943 pages).
5. Some `{{fonte}}` titles are in English although the template is not `{{fonteestera}}` (e.g. Euronews in English); Gioia's brief drops them from the `fonti` test set with a rule on English function words (G2, not built yet).
6. Printed sources (`{{fontecarta}}`) are not parsed: they are not in the contract.
